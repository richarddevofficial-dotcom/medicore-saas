⚠️ Critical: Payment Gates - PAYMENT BEFORE SERVICE

The system requires payment at these 4 stages:

  • 🔴 Gate 1: Consultation Fee → Before Doctor sees patient
  • 🔴 Gate 2: Lab Test Fees → If Lab Tests ordered
  • 🔴 Gate 3: Imaging Fees → If Imaging ordered
  • 🔴 Gate 4: Medicine Cost → Before Pharmacy dispenses

✓ No payment = No service proceeds to next stage

📊 Detailed Process Stages

Stage 1️⃣: Reception

Patient arrives and visits reception desk. Staff collect basic information and check if new or existing patient.

Role: Receptionist/Admin

Stage 2️⃣: Registration

Create patient record in system. Collect full details, insurance info, and assign unique MRN (Medical Record Number).

Role: Receptionist/Admin

Stage 3️⃣: Payment Gate 1

Patient pays consultation fee at cashier. NO payment = patient cannot see doctor.

Role: Cashier/Finance

Stage 4️⃣: Doctor Consultation

Doctor examines patient, listens to complaints, checks vital signs, and makes initial diagnosis.

Role: Doctor

Stage 5️⃣: Doctor's Decision

Doctor decides if lab tests or imaging needed. Patient may have only consultation, or need additional tests.

Role: Doctor

Stage 6A️⃣: Lab Tests (Optional)

If doctor orders: Patient gives blood sample. Lab technician performs tests and sends results back to doctor.

Role: Lab Technician

Stage 6B️⃣: Imaging (Optional)

If doctor orders: Patient gets X-ray/CT/Ultrasound. Radiographer captures images and sends to doctor.

Role: Radiographer

Stage 7️⃣: Doctor Reviews

Doctor reviews all test results (if any) and finalizes diagnosis. Issues prescription with medicine names and dosage.

Role: Doctor

Stage 8️⃣: Payment Gate 2

Patient pays for medicines at cashier. NO payment = pharmacist won't dispense medicines.

Role: Cashier/Finance

Stage 9️⃣: Pharmacy Dispensation

Pharmacist checks prescription, verifies medicines, counts tablets, labels bottles, and hands to patient with instructions.

Role: Pharmacist

Stage 🔟: Exit

Patient leaves hospital with medicines, receipt, and instructions. Journey complete!

Role: Finance/Reception

🔒 Data Privacy & Compliance

HIPAA Compliant: Each role sees only what they need:

  • ✓ Receptionist → Patient contact info only
  • ✓ Doctor → Full medical history
  • ✓ Pharmacist → Medicines ONLY (not diagnosis)
  • ✓ Finance → Payments only (not medical)
  • ✓ Lab Tech → Lab results only
