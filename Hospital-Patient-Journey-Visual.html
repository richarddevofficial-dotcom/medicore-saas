🏥 Hospital Patient Journey

Complete workflow from Registration to Pharmacy with Payment Gates

1

👤 Patient Arrives at Reception

What happens: Patient comes to hospital and goes to reception desk

Actions:

  • Receive patient & welcome
  • Get patient information
  • Check if new or existing patient
👤 Receptionist / Admin
⬇️
2

📋 Patient Registration

What happens: Create patient record in system

Data Collected:

  • Full name, age, gender
  • Phone, address
  • Insurance details & policy number
  • Medical history
  • Assign MRN (Medical Record Number)
👤 Receptionist / Admin
⬇️
💰

💳 Payment 1: Consultation Fee

⚠️ PAYMENT REQUIRED BEFORE CONSULTATION

Amount: Doctor consultation fee (e.g., $50)

Payment Methods:

  • 💵 Cash
  • 💳 Credit/Debit Card
  • 🏦 Insurance Coverage (if applicable)

✓ Once payment confirmed → Proceed to Doctor

💰 Cashier / Finance
⬇️
3

👨‍⚕️ Doctor Consultation

What happens: Patient sees doctor for evaluation

Doctor Actions:

  • Review patient history
  • Listen to complaints
  • Physical examination
  • Take vital signs (BP, temp, etc.)
  • Make initial diagnosis
👨‍⚕️ Doctor
⬇️

🔀 Doctor Decision: Further Investigation Needed?

Doctor decides based on symptoms and examination

🧪 Option A: Laboratory Tests

Blood tests, urine tests, chemistry panel

📸 Option B: Imaging

X-ray, CT scan, Ultrasound

👇 Or Both | Or Direct to Prescription 👇

⬇️
4A

🧪 Laboratory Tests (If Ordered)

What happens: Patient gives blood sample for testing

Lab Tech Actions:

  • Call patient name
  • Collect blood sample
  • Perform tests (CBC, UA, Chemistry)
  • Record results
  • Send results to doctor (24-48 hours)
🧪 Lab Technician
⬇️
💰

💳 Payment 2: Lab Tests Fee

⚠️ PAYMENT REQUIRED FOR LAB TESTS

Amount: Lab test charges (varies by tests)

💰 Cashier / Finance
4B

📸 Imaging/Radiology (If Ordered)

What happens: Patient gets X-ray, CT, or Ultrasound

Radiographer Actions:

  • Call patient name
  • Position patient for imaging
  • Perform X-ray / CT / Ultrasound
  • Capture images
  • Send images & report to doctor
📸 Radiographer
⬇️
💰

💳 Payment 3: Imaging Fee

⚠️ PAYMENT REQUIRED FOR IMAGING

Amount: Imaging charges (X-ray/CT/Ultrasound)

💰 Cashier / Finance
⬇️ Results Back to Doctor ⬇️
5

📋 Doctor Reviews & Prescribes Medicine

What happens: Doctor reviews all test results and issues prescription

Doctor Actions:

  • Review lab results (if done)
  • Review imaging (if done)
  • Finalize diagnosis
  • Select appropriate medicine
  • Specify dosage (e.g., 500mg twice daily)
  • Specify duration (e.g., 7 days)
  • Send prescription to pharmacy
👨‍⚕️ Doctor
⬇️
💰

💳 Payment 4: Medicine Cost

⚠️ PAYMENT REQUIRED BEFORE PHARMACY DISPENSES

Amount: Cost of medicines (varies by prescription)

Price breakdown:

  • Medicine 1: $15
  • Medicine 2: $20
  • Packaging: $2

✓ Once payment confirmed → Proceed to Pharmacy

💰 Cashier / Finance
⬇️
6

🏥 Pharmacy Dispensation

What happens: Pharmacist prepares and gives medicine to patient

Pharmacist Actions:

  • Receive prescription from doctor
  • Verify prescription validity
  • Check for drug interactions
  • Check medicine availability & expiry
  • Count correct quantity
  • Label bottles with patient name
  • Package medicines properly
  • Provide usage instructions
  • Hand package to patient
💊 Pharmacist
⬇️
7

📄 Final Receipt & Summary

Patient Receives:

  • ✓ Medicines (in proper packaging)
  • ✓ Medicine instructions & warnings
  • ✓ Doctor's prescription copy
  • ✓ Lab results (if done)
  • ✓ Imaging report (if done)
  • ✓ Final itemized receipt

Total Amount Paid: [Consultation + Lab + Imaging + Medicines]

💰 Finance
⬇️
✓

👋 Patient Leaves Hospital

Journey Complete!

Patient follows medicine instructions at home and recovers.
